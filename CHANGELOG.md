# Changelog

## 1.0 — October 2026

First release.

- A research report built from what you type, as US Letter pages, saved as a PDF through the browser's print dialog, with these parts:
  - a branded cover with the question, depth rating, confidence, and source count
  - a contents page with page numbers and a list of figures and tables
  - a one-page bottom line: summary, depth rating from 1 to 5 with its dimensions, recommended treatment, research basis, overall confidence, key takeaways with their own confidence, and decisions for the reader
  - key considerations with labels in the margin, and common misconceptions against what the research shows
  - the research in numbered sections and subsections, with footnotes, charts, tables, key points, margin notes, and labeled rows
  - debates, each with its views and an assessment, and open questions with what would settle them
  - reference: key actors, a timeline, and terms
  - a source assessment: counts, sources grouped as core, supporting, or further reading, each with its grade and annotation, and a short grade key
  - lettered appendices, with the Admiralty source rating key available as a table
  - an "About this report" page with method, handling, a reference line, version history, disclaimer, and logo
- A brand kept apart from the report: organization, tagline, logo, report name, number prefix, handling marking, accent and ink colors, and page color. It carries over to new reports and saves to its own file.
- Running headers with the report name, number, and section; footers with the short title, the handling marking, and the page number.
- Citations from OSINT Citation Generator list files, as in the White Paper Builder, in every part of the report.
- Opens White Paper Builder projects.
- Checks for missing titles, unrated depth, a bottom line longer than a page, debates without an assessment, ungraded or unannotated sources, unknown source keys, figures without sources, and brand colors too faint to read.
- Projects save to a file and reopen on any computer. Work in progress is kept in the browser. Nothing is sent anywhere.
